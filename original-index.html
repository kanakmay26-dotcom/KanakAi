Why automate

Built for the hours you're not watching

A strategy is only as good as its execution. Kanak AI removes hesitation, revenge trades, and screen fatigue from the equation — it runs the same logic at 3am as it does at 3pm.

Range-breakout core

02

Adaptive confirmation

03
