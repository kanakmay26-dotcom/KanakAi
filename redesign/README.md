Buildless static site in `dist/`. This is a separate owner-private preview; the existing Vercel deployments and payment APIs are unchanged.

Sq9 Origin prices supplied by owner: $25 USD/month and $311 USD/year. These are presented as supplied, without an annual discount claim. Plan enquiries open the visitor's email app; no email is sent automatically. Included access, activation, renewal and cancellation terms require confirmation with support.

The new catalogue preserves the verified Kanak AI and Kanak Algo V1 prices and links existing paid-product checkout to the original website. It does not migrate payment APIs into the preview.

Sq9 live terminal inspection on 14 October 2026 showed an example anchor and awaiting ephemeris data. The supplied Pine v8 uses JPL approximate orbital elements plus a separate lunar series, not DE442s. Its external event-input compatibility is not verified. The calculator implements only the supplied geometric level formula, with automatic price scaling; it is not a live market feed or astronomy engine.
